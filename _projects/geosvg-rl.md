GeoSVG-RL studies layout-constrained text-to-SVG diagram generation through geometry-aware reinforcement learning. The project focuses on controllable visual structure, spatial relations, and executable vector outputs.

- Paper: [arXiv:2605.25447](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25447)
- Direction: reinforcement learning, diagram generation, SVG, layout constraints
