About Me

Sifan Li

Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science

sflijohn@foxmail.com | johnnyzeppelin.github.io | github.com/johnnyZeppelin | scholar:Gc1bAVwAAAAJ

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the supervision of Prof. Shenghua Liu. I am also an assistant researcher in AI4Science at Shanghai AI Lab and a research intern at the University of California, Merced, working under the supervision of Prof. Yiwei Wang. My research interests include NLP, multimodal LLMs, VLMs, AI interpretability, agentic systems, reinforcement learning, AI4Science, efficient training, and computer vision.
